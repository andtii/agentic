Rules the platform enforces
Assigned is a queue, claimed is work in progress. An agent can have many assigned items but only its limit claimed at once (1 by default).
A lease runs out if the agent goes quiet; the item goes back to the top of its queue and Atlas is told.
An item can’t be claimed until the items it comes after are done.
Items declare the paths they touch. Overlaps warn both agents and suggest an order instead of a merge conflict.
Done means every done-when is ticked, or you mark it done yourself.
Every change is in History with who did it, so you can replay how the plan moved.
